Case 1: The Stolen Cursed Sapphire

Case Description

A group of friends were exploring an old abandon manor together when the found a Cursed Sapphire. They decided they would return together and give it to Memory at the return room. When they got back though, they realized the Sapphire was gone. They know no one dropped it so it seems one of them must have taken it.
