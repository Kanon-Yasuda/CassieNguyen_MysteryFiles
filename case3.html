Case 3: The Missing Cat

Case Description

Mr. Strawberry, a prized cat, has gone missing from the Cat Cabby Showcase. He was a strange-looking cat who looked just like a strawberry, making him extremely rare and worth a lot of money. There were many people visiting the Cat Cabby Studio that day, so no one knows who could have taken him. After his disappearance was discovered, the doors were sealed to prevent anyone from leaving. Everyone who was inside the building when Mr. Strawberry disappeared is still there. Now it is up to you to find out who stole him.

Suspects

Lily

Lily

"I was the one taking care of Mr. Strawberry before the showcase started. He was such a sweet little cat, even though he looked a bit strange. I made sure he had his food and water before I left him in the display room. When I came back, he was gone! I really hope we can find him soon. He must be so scared right now..."

Oliver

Oliver

"I came here because I heard there was a cat that looked like a strawberry. I must admit, he was quite a fascinating little creature. I even offered to buy him, but the owner refused. What a shame. Still, I have plenty of rare animals at home, so I don't see why I would need to steal another one."

Emma

Emma

"I was just here to take pictures for the showcase. There were so many cute cats that I honestly didn't know which one to photograph first! I did take a few pictures of Mr. Strawberry, but I left the display room when the staff told everyone to prepare for the next event. I was in the photography room editing my pictures when they announced he was missing."

Felix

Felix

"Listen, I don't know anything about the missing cat. I was busy moving supplies around the studio all afternoon. I did go into the display room earlier, but that was only to bring some decorations. Besides, I never even touched Mr. Strawberry. I don't know why everyone keeps asking me about him."

Evidence

Clue 1: Showcase Records

Not Reviewed

Mr. Strawberry was last seen inside his display room shortly before the showcase began. Lily had finished feeding him, and Emma had taken several photographs of him. The staff later discovered that his display was empty, but the door showed no signs of being forced open.

Clue 2: Security Records

Not Reviewed

The studio's security cameras showed Oliver speaking with the owner near the entrance. Emma was seen entering the photography room, while Lily was helping another staff member prepare the other cats. Felix was recorded pushing a large supply cart out of the display room shortly before Mr. Strawberry disappeared.

Clue 3: Supply Room Records

Not Reviewed

During the investigation, the staff discovered a small amount of strawberry-colored fur inside an empty supply box. The box had several air holes and was found on the same cart Felix had been using. According to the studio records, Felix was the only person assigned to move that cart during the showcase.
